## Collection concept

**VOL. 01 — The Off-Season** is a tight, collectible first drop for people who are *professionally off-duty*. Every piece reads as issued kit of the **Private Leisure Department** — quiet, well-made, and dryly funny in the labelling rather than the graphics.

**Strategic role of the drop:** prove PICKLE is (a) genuinely well-made and (b) a *world*, not a graphic-tee shop. It must feel like **one small wardrobe you could pack for a slow week** — 7 pieces, no filler, everything mixes.

**Range (7 styles):** 4 T-shirt designs · 2 caps (the hero) · 1 short. No hoodies, jackets, trousers, bags, socks or accessories (per foundation).

## Colour strategy

- **Spine (repeats every season):** Bottle Green `#24382C`, Bisque `#EDE4CF`, Club Navy `#1F2A44`.
- **Rotating warm accents:** Chalk, Sage, Olive, Terracotta, Sea Blue.
- **The single loud colour:** **Tomato `#D14B3C`** — appears on the Athletic tee *only*, as punctuation.
- **The rule:** every colour pairs with the spine and with each other; no colour exists only to fill a grid.

## Graphic strategy

Restraint plus one earned statement. Marks are quiet by default (chest emblem, cap front, hem, leg). The one loud graphic per drop is the **Department back-print**. All copy-driven — no illustration, no literal pickles.

---

## How it wears (wardrobe logic)

- **Everyday backbone:** Issue Tee + Off-Duty Short + Member Cap. *(Bisque tee, olive short, green cap.)*
- **The statement:** one loud tee, everything else quiet. *(Tomato Athletic tee, navy cap, bisque short.)*
- **The quiet flex (Aarav):** all-tonal, marks barely there. *(Faded-black Issue tee, black short, grey cap.)*
- **The entry (Kabir):** start with a cap, graduate to a tee. *(Green Member Cap → Issue Tee.)*

Each of the three foundation archetypes has an obvious, affordable way in — and a reason to come back.

---

## Buy-plan hypothesis

Over-index on **caps** (entry + reach, lowest return risk) and the **Issue Tee** in Bisque/Navy (backbone). Treat the **Tomato Athletic tee** as a limited hero — deliberately under-buy so it sells out and seeds demand. **Shorts:** modest first buy, learn the fit before scaling. *(All quantities to be set with real margin targets.)*
