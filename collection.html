02 / Colour Story

The Drop's Palette

A disciplined core carries the drop; warm accents rotate; exactly one loud colour. Everything mixes with everything.

The rule: Bisque, bottle green and navy are the spine (they repeat every season). Sage, olive, terracotta, sea blue and chalk are the rotating warm accents. Tomato is the single loud colour — it appears on one tee only, as punctuation. No colour exists just to fill a grid.

05 / Notes

What's Set, What's Open

This is the design + merchandising direction. Fabric specs, patterns and sampling follow your sign-off.

✓ Recommended

  • 7-piece drop: 4 tees, 2 caps, 1 short. No filler.
  • Caps as hero/entry; Issue Tee as backbone; Department Tee as flagship.
  • Tomato used once, on the Athletic tee, as the single loud note.
  • Archive-style SKU coding (VOL·CAT·DESIGN·COLOUR).
  • Under-buy the statement tee; over-buy caps + Issue Tee.

◐ Exploratory

  • Fabric weights (tees ~240gsm, etc.) are placeholders — confirm with the maker.
  • Prices are hypotheses — validate against landed cost + positioning.
  • Cap construction: structured 6-panel vs. unstructured — sample both.
  • Short length (~7") and closure (drawcord vs. elastic).
  • Whether Department back-print runs large or mid-scale.

⧉ Needs approval

  • Sign off the 7-style lineup and roles.
  • Confirm colourway list per style.
  • Approve price architecture (or give target margins).
  • Green-light tech-pack + sampling for the hero pieces first.
  • Then: photography brief + the e-commerce build.
