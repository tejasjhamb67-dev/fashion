## Stage 1 — Foundation rules carried forward (visual only)

Extracted from `PICKLE-Brand-Foundation.md` — only the decisions that bind visual identity:

1. **Straight face.** The name is silly; execution never is. No literal pickles, jars, dill puns, cartoon vegetables, "big dill". This is rule zero.
2. **The moat is humour + world, not palette.** The *coastal old-money* aesthetic (Sporty & Rich, Aimé Leon Dore territory) is **crowded**; a pretty version of it reads derivative. Distinctiveness must come from the **name owned with confidence** + a **proprietary brand world** + **deadpan copy**.
3. **Caps are a hero category** (per moodboard evidence), not an accessory. The identity must survive 4mm cap embroidery.
4. **Quiet by default.** Personality scores: Premium 8, Quiet 3, Refined 3. Small marks, one loud moment per drop, restraint everywhere.
5. **Humour lives in language, naming, styling and details** — never in front-of-chest gags.
6. **No invented heritage** — no founding year, awards, or unproven claims. Use archive/member numbers and "since recently" instead of a fake "Est. 19XX".
7. **Warm, sun-faded palette** as the emotional base — but not "all beige".
8. **The Club pillar** — PICKLE is a place, not a print; buyers are "members".

## Stage 2 — Three creative routes

### Route A — Riviera Club
- **Creative idea:** a refined Mediterranean leisure club (Capri / Amalfi / Monaco, Riva boats, tennis & sailing, vintage resort graphics).
- **Feeling:** golden-hour ease, tanned, quietly expensive.
- **Visual personality:** elegant, warm, resort-preppy.
- **Colour:** cream `#EFE7D6`, sea blue `#3E7CB1`, navy `#182338`, tomato `#D14B3C`, brass `#C79A3E`.
- **Typography:** Fraunces (elegant high-contrast serif) + Archivo.
- **Logo direction:** serif wordmark + "Pickle Leisure Club" descriptor; sailing-flag striping; resort crest.
- **Graphic language:** flag stripes, racket/anchor motifs, resort crest, script accents.
- **Photography:** sun-faded film, boats, harbours, poolside, candid.
- **Product application:** striped caps, resort-crest tees, woven flag labels.
- **Why it could work:** instantly premium and wearable, straight off the moodboard.
- **Main risk:** it *is* the crowded lane — without wit it's a beautiful copy of Sporty & Rich.
- **Recommendation:** keep as a **seasonal sub-lane / fallback aesthetic**, not the house.

### Route B — Oddly Official  ★ RECOMMENDED
- **Creative idea:** a believable private institution — the "Private Leisure Department" — devoted, with total sincerity, to doing very little. Fake club, official seals, membership cards, archive labels, dry bureaucratic language.
- **Feeling:** deadpan, dependable, quietly amused. The straightest face in the room.
- **Visual personality:** institutional, archival, ink-on-paper, warm.
- **Colour:** bisque `#F1E8D4`, **bottle green `#24382C`**, ink `#211E18`, **stamp oxblood `#A5382A`**, **brass `#A8823C`**.
- **Typography:** Archivo (institutional grotesque) + Fraunces (warmth/voice) + Space Mono (codes/labels).
- **Logo direction:** confident grotesque wordmark + "PRIVATE LEISURE DEPT." descriptor; circular club emblem; rubber-stamp system.
- **Graphic language:** rubber stamps ("FOR RECREATIONAL USE"), reg./member numbers, department labels, the seal, dry sub-lines.
- **Photography:** *borrowed from Riviera* — sun-faded film keeps it from reading cold.
- **Product application:** "MEMBER" / "OFF DUTY" caps, department back-prints, membership hangtags, stamped mailers.
- **Why it could work:** owns territory no one else does; turns the silly name into the whole thesis; humour is infinitely extendable through copy, not cartoons; delivers the foundation's "Club" + "Straight Face" pillars directly.
- **Main risk:** played cold it reads nerdy — must stay **warm and beautifully made**.
- **Recommendation:** **the house identity.**

### Route C — Analog Leisure
- **Creative idea:** record-store / film-camera nostalgia — vinyl, cassettes, old music labels, printed ephemera, 70s–80s leisure graphics.
- **Feeling:** slow Sunday, good record, window open. Warm, unhurried, creative.
- **Visual personality:** eccentric, tactile, culturally curious.
- **Colour:** paper `#EEE3CB`, faded teal `#2F6E6A`, terracotta `#C0663C`, mustard `#DDB23B`, vinyl black `#241F17`.
- **Typography:** Fraunces (soft/italic) + Space Mono, heavy on print-label feel.
- **Logo direction:** record-label ring, tracklist type, tape-spine lockups.
- **Graphic language:** label rings, tracklists, tape spines, poster type.
- **Photography:** warm grain, still-life ephemera, interiors.
- **Product application:** tracklist back-prints, label-ring caps, tape-spine hem labels, posters.
- **Why it could work:** genuinely distinctive, content-rich, community-friendly.
- **Main risk:** tips retro/costume-y fast; reads less premium if craft slips.
- **Recommendation:** keep for a **one-off capsule / collab**, not the house.

## Stage 3 — The decision

**Chosen: Route B — Oddly Official**, warmed by Route A's sun-faded palette & film photography, and textured with a few of Route C's archive/label details.

**Why it's strongest:**
- **Distinctiveness:** it's the only route that turns the biggest liability (a silly name) into the entire creative engine. "A serious institution for not being serious" is ownable; a beautiful coastal palette is not.
- **Premium perception:** ink-on-paper archival systems (seals, reg. numbers, department labels, restrained type) read expensive and considered — and the warm palette keeps it human.
- **Wearability:** the actual garments stay quiet and classic (small marks, one back-print) — the world lives in *language and detail*, so people wear it without wearing a joke.
- **Commercial potential:** the humour is infinitely extendable through copy (new stamps, new departments, new member language every drop) at zero illustration cost, and it makes caps — the hero category — effortless ("MEMBER", "OFF DUTY", "PKL").

**Why the others are weaker:** Route A walks straight into the most saturated aesthetic in fashion and depends on wit it doesn't structurally contain. Route C is distinctive but fights the premium goal and drifts retro.

**Borrowed from the rejected routes:**
- From **Riviera:** the sun-faded warm palette, the film-photography direction, coastal-leisure settings (this is what keeps Official from feeling cold).
- From **Analog:** archive/catalogue numbering, printed-ephemera texture, the "Vol. / Side" collection language.

**Final direction (one line):** *PICKLE — Private Leisure Dept. A straight-faced institution for the well-made and the off-duty, rendered ink-on-warm-paper.*

## Stage 5 — Typography system

Three families — practical for garments, web, packaging, labels, social, email and photo overlays.

| Role | Typeface | Substitute if unlicensed | Use | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Display** | **Archivo** (Extra-Bold 800) | Anton, Roboto Condensed, "Helvetica Now Display" | Wordmark, headlines, back-prints, cap embroidery | UPPERCASE, tracking +2–6%, tight leading |
| **Serif / voice** | **Fraunces** (500, italic) | Recoleta, "GT Sectra", Georgia | Statements, quotes, warmth, thank-you cards | Sentence case, italic for voice, never all-caps |
| **Body** | **Archivo** (400/500) | Inter, Söhne, system-ui | Product copy, web, email | Sentence case, ~65ch measure, line-height 1.6 |
| **Utility** | **Space Mono** (400/700) | IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono | Labels, product/archive codes, member numbers | UPPERCASE, tracking +14–20%, tabular numerals |

- **Hierarchy:** Display (identity) → Serif (voice) → Body (information) → Mono (paperwork).
- **Capitalisation:** Display & Mono = UPPERCASE; Serif & Body = sentence case; never all-caps a Fraunces line.
- **Italic** only in Fraunces (the "voice"). **Condensed/expanded** not used — Archivo's default width is the system.
- All are **Google Fonts** (open-licence) so nothing falls back silently; every stack declares a real fallback.

## Stage 7 — Graphic language

Proprietary devices (the recognisable PICKLE toolkit):
1. **The club emblem** — circular seal with rotating member/vol. copy.
2. **Rubber stamps** — "FOR RECREATIONAL USE", "OFFICIALLY OFF DUTY", slightly rotated, oxblood or ink.
3. **Registration / member numbers** — `REG. NO. PKL-01`, `MEMBER No. 0042`, hand-numbered feel, Space Mono.
4. **Department labels** — "PRIVATE LEISURE DEPT.", "NON-COMPETITIVE SPORTS DIVISION".
5. **Boxed monogram** — PKL in a double-rule box.
6. **Dry sub-lines** — a deadpan descriptor under the wordmark.
7. **Collection coding** — "VOL. 01 — The Off-Season", "Side B".

**Compositions:** small left-chest emblem (tee front); centred department back-print (tee back, one loud piece per drop); brass-on-green cap front + "PKL" side; tiny leg-hit on shorts.
**Avoid:** literal pickles, "big dill", cartoon vegetables, palm trees, flames, smileys, fake luxury symbols, over-complex illustration, Sporty & Rich copies.
